Clea Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Clea (Google Cloud app "Clea Hermes Google Access") is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Romello Wakefield. It is not offered to the public.

What Clea accesses

With the owner's permission, Clea uses Google's APIs to read, label and send email in the owner's Gmail account and to read and manage the owner's Google Calendar. It accesses no other Google accounts.

How the data is used

Gmail and Calendar data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks for, such as tracking replies to job applications and planning study time. It is processed on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Clea does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. It is not used for advertising and is not used to train AI models.

Storage and security

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner's account.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact

Romello Wakefield: Romello.wakefield@gmail.com

Clea's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
